About Card Block

A free, open-data tool to help Indian consumers quickly find card blocking numbers when their credit or debit cards are lost or stolen.

Every year, millions of bank cards in India are misplaced, lost, or stolen. The first 60 minutes after losing a card are critical — but most people waste time searching for the right toll-free number. Card Block solves this by providing a single, verified source of truth.

Our Mission

We believe financial emergency information should be free, open, and instantly accessible. Card Block is an open data project maintained by Cashless Consumer — a non-profit consumer advocacy group working to protect digital payment users in India.

How to Contribute

Card Block is open-source under the MIT License. You can help by:

  • Submitting updated bank numbers via Cashless Consumer
  • Adding new banks to the dataset
  • Contributing code on GitHub
  • Reporting outdated or incorrect information
