About

What is Card Block API?

Card Block API is an open database of card blocking information for banks worldwide. It helps humans and AI agents quickly find how to block a lost or stolen debit or credit card.

Started in 2026 as part of the CashlessConsumer initiative to improve consumer safety in India's digital payment ecosystem.

Why This Project?

When a card is lost or stolen, consumers need immediate access to blocking information. Banks often make this difficult to find, burying it in complex websites or requiring customers to call during limited hours.

This project:

  • Makes card blocking information easily accessible
  • Provides verified information from official sources
  • Supports both human users and AI agents
  • Follows RBI guidelines for consumer protection
